📢 Announcement: BNS, BNSS & BSA Now Available!

New Legal Resources Added to Nyaya Setu

📅 11 August 2026

We are pleased to announce that the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS), Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita (BNSS), and Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam (BSA) are now available on Nyaya Setu.

Explore these important Indian laws through our digital legal resources section.

📚 Now Available:

• ⚖️ BNS — Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023

• 🏛️ BNSS — Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023

• 📜 BSA — Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023

Our goal is to make legal information more accessible and convenient for students, advocates, researchers, and everyone interested in Indian law.

Visit Nyaya Setu and explore the newly available legal resources today!
